## 1. Executive Color Philosophy

The **HAS NAA** color palette embodies the quintessential *Quiet Luxury* aesthetic: an intimate dialogue between warm organic earths, luminous desert sand, refined metallic undertones, and deep architectural carbon. 

Rooted in the natural majesty of Mediterranean and Egyptian sunlight—natural unbleached Egyptian cotton, fine raw silk, alabaster stone, and artisanal Florentine leather—the palette shuns loud, seasonal neons in favor of timeless longevity.

## 3. The 60-30-10 Luxury Distribution Rule

In luxury fashion art direction, balance and optical tranquility are paramount. The palette must never appear noisy or cluttered.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 60%  DOMINANT BACKGROUND                               │
│      Primary Ivory (#F5F0E8)                           │
│      Editorial canvas, negative space, packaging linen │
├──────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────┤
│ 30%  TYPOGRAPHY & STRUCTURE      │ 10% ACCENTS         │
│      Charcoal (#292522)          │     Champagne Beige │
│      Mocha Brown (#6B5140)       │     (#DCC8AC)       │
│      Headings, copy, key buttons │     Warm Taupe      │
└──────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────┘
```

1. **60% Primary Canvas (Ivory #F5F0E8 & Off-White #FAF7F2):**
   Provides breathing room for fashion photography and garment silhouettes. Evokes calm, high-end gallery space.
2. **30% Structure & Text (Charcoal #292522 & Mocha #6B5140):**
   Delivers authoritative, razor-sharp editorial legibility. Avoids eye strain while commanding visual respect.
3. **10% Accents & Details (Champagne #DCC8AC & Warm Taupe #A68B70):**
   Reserved exclusively for delicate hair-lines, active indicators, monogram seals, metallic foil stamping, and status badges.

## 4. Accessibility & Contrast Matrices (WCAG 2.1 Standards)

All digital touchpoints strictly adhere to international accessibility benchmarks while maintaining haute-couture aesthetics.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Charcoal (`#292522`)** | Primary Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) | **14.28 : 1** | **AAA (Pass)** | Master Body Text, Headings, Navigation |
| **Mocha Brown (`#6B5140`)** | Primary Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) | **5.45 : 1** | **AA (Pass)** | Subheadings, Descriptors, Medium Labels |
| **Warm Taupe (`#A68B70`)** | Primary Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) | **2.68 : 1** | *Decorative* | Hairline Borders, Dividers, Inactive Icons Only |
| **Primary Ivory (`#F5F0E8`)** | Charcoal (`#292522`) | **14.28 : 1** | **AAA (Pass)** | Primary Buttons, Dark Mode Text, Footer Copy |
| **Champagne Beige (`#DCC8AC`)**| Charcoal (`#292522`) | **8.12 : 1** | **AAA (Pass)** | Dark Mode Accents, Foiled Logos, Buttons |
| **Champagne Beige (`#DCC8AC`)**| Mocha Brown (`#6B5140`) | **3.85 : 1** | **AA Large Text**| Large Badges, Decorative Overlays |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never use Champagne Beige (`#DCC8AC`) for body text or small copy against Primary Ivory (`#F5F0E8`), as the contrast ratio is below 1.5:1. Champagne Beige is strictly an accent, metallic foil, or decorative hairline element.

## 7. Physical Production, Materials & Packaging Guide

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Approved Packaging Finish Directives:**
> 1. **Primary Finish:** **Matte Blind Deboss** is the master packaging finish. All primary branding on shopping bags, rigid gift boxes, and stationery must utilize tone-on-tone blind deboss/emboss on unbleached Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) or Champagne (`#DCC8AC`) substrates.
> 2. **Secondary Metallic Accent:** Champagne Pale Gold (`Pantone 871 C` / metallic foil) may *only* be used as a delicate secondary accent on selected premium details (e.g. ribbon edge, micro-fine card borders, interior authenticity seals), never as the dominant packaging finish.

| Medium | Recommended Material | Color Formulation | Finishing Technique |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shopping Bags** | 250gsm FSC-certified uncoated Ivory art paper | Base: `#F5F0E8` (Ivory) | **Matte Blind Deboss** logo + tone-on-tone descriptor (Quiet Luxury) |
| **Rigid Gift Boxes** | Recycled rigid board wrapped in unbleached Ivory linen | Exterior: `#F5F0E8` / Interior: `#FAF7F2` | **Matte Blind Deboss** HN monogram; optional Champagne micro-accent edge |
| **Garment Hang Tags**| 600gsm duplex board (Ivory front / Taupe back) | Ivory `#F5F0E8` & Charcoal `#292522` | Deep letterpress deboss + raw Taupe cotton cord |
| **Tissue Paper** | 18gsm semi-translucent crisp unbleached tissue | Ground: `#F5F0E8` / Pattern: `#EAE3D5` | Subtle tone-on-tone HN monogram watermark |
| **Ribbon** | 25mm double-faced woven grosgrain or silk | Champagne Beige `#DCC8AC` | Charcoal screen-printed wordmark with micro-spacing |
| **Boutique Signage** | Brushed bronze / matte champagne architectural metal | Champagne `#DCC8AC` on Alabaster/Granite | Subtle matte relief with warm 2700K rear halo illumination |

## 8. Brand Color Rules & Misuse Prohibitions

- **DO NOT** use Champagne Pale Gold foil as a primary packaging finish. Primary finishes must remain understated Matte Blind Deboss.
- **DO NOT** use pure saturated RGB black (`#000000`). Always use rich Charcoal (`#292522`).
- **DO NOT** introduce neon, bright pink, electric purple, or saturated primary tones (red, blue, yellow) under any circumstance.
- **DO NOT** apply gradients across the logo or wordmark. Keep color fills matte, flat, and architectural.
- **DO NOT** place low-contrast text combinations (e.g., Champagne on Ivory, or Taupe on Mocha).
- **DO NOT** use glossy synthetic finishes in print packaging. Always insist on tactile matte, soft-touch, or linen-textured substrates.
