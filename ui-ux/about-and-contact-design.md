### 1.1 Sectional Breakdown

#### Section 1: Editorial Prologue
- **Visual:** Full-width atmospheric image of an artisan in the Zamalek atelier draping raw ivory silk over a dress form in morning sunlight.
- **Headline (Cormorant Garamond 44px):**
  - EN: *"Elegance is not about being noticed, but being remembered."*
  - AR: *"الأناقة ليست في لفت الأنظار، بل في البقاء في الذاكرة."*
- **Kicker:** Montserrat 11px uppercase, tracked `0.45em`: *"THE HOUSE OF HAS NAA — ESTABLISHED 2026"*

#### Section 2: The Meaning of HAS NAA (حسناء)
- **Concept:** An intimate typographic exploration of the Arabic word *Hasnaa*.
- **Layout:** Centered Cormorant Garamond display paired with authentic Arabic calligraphy from the *Amiri* family.
- **Copy:**
  - *“In the classical Arabic lexicon, Hasnaa (حسناء) describes more than physical beauty; it embodies grace of spirit, dignified presence, and natural charm that requires no ornamentation. We chose this name as an architectural foundation for an identity built on understated feminine power.”*

#### Section 3: Cairo & Alexandria as Creative Muses (Dual Editorial Split)
- **Left Column:** *The Sunlit Modernity of Cairo* (Neoclassical Zamalek facades, sandstone minimalism, cosmopolitan evenings).
- **Right Column:** *The Mediterranean Poise of Alexandria* (Breezy seaside linen, antique European romance, refined ease).
- **Photography:** 35mm film-toned black-and-white and warm sepia photography capturing the spirit of modern Egypt.

#### Section 4: The Atelier & Master Craftsmanship
- **Visual Showcase:** Quad-grid displaying the tactile realities of creation:
  1. Hand-drafted wooden pattern grading and chalk tailoring lines.
  2. Spools of pure silk thread in Champagne and Mocha tones.
  3. Master tailors inspecting hand-stitched French seams and blind hems.
  4. The finishing touch: stitching the signature damask woven silk label.
- **Copy:** Highlighting that every piece is constructed by Egyptian master tailors with decades of haute-couture training, ensuring garments drape effortlessly for a lifetime.

#### Section 5: Textile Purity & Ethical Commitment
- **Three Material Pillars:**
  1. **Giza 45 Extra-Long Staple Cotton:** Revered globally as the finest cotton on Earth, grown in the Nile Delta, harvested by hand, offering unmatched silk-like softness and durability.
  2. **100% Pure Mulberry Silk Crepe:** Natural, breathable, and drape-retaining; free from synthetic polyester blends.
  3. **Unbleached Egyptian Linen:** Structured yet soft, perfect for Mediterranean and desert climates.
- **Anti-Fast Fashion Philosophy:** Zero mass production. Every design is produced in deliberate limited capsules of 15 to 40 pieces to eliminate textile waste.

### 2.2 Right Column: Online Message & Inquiry Form
- **Form Architecture:**
  1. `Client Full Name` (Input with clean bottom hairline border).
  2. `Phone / WhatsApp Number` (Pre-formatted with Egyptian `+20` country code).
  3. `Email Address` (For written confirmation).
  4. `Inquiry Topic:`
     - `Product Sizing & Fit Guidance`
     - `Fabric & Care Questions`
     - `Ordering via Courier`
     - `General Inquiry`
  5. `Message Box:`
     - Client notes, specific piece references, or questions.
- **Submission Action:**
  - Full-width Charcoal button: `SEND MESSAGE TO CONCIERGE` (`إرسال الرسالة إلى خدمة العملاء`).
  - Response: Instant elegant confirmation feedback with direct option to continue on WhatsApp.

### 2.3 Frequently Asked Concierge Inquiries (Accordion FAQ)
Located directly below the contact form to resolve client questions with elegance:

1. **How do I order or inquire about a piece online?**  
   *Simply tap the "Inquire via WhatsApp" button on any product card or details page. Our styling team will confirm your size, share video close-ups if desired, and arrange convenient courier delivery across Egypt.*
2. **Where does HAS NAA deliver?**  
   *We provide white-glove courier delivery across all Egyptian governorates, with delivery typically within 24 to 48 hours in Cairo and Giza, and 48 to 72 hours across other governorates.*
3. **What payment methods are supported for orders?**  
   *Orders arranged through our WhatsApp concierge support Cash on Delivery (COD) across Egypt, as well as instant bank transfer and digital wallets.*
4. **How do I ensure the correct size?**  
   *You can review our interactive Size Guide on any product page, or message our WhatsApp team with your measurements (bust, waist, hips, height) for personalized guidance.*
